## Metadata policy

References used as methodological evidence in the state-of-the-art vignette have been checked against two sources as of **2026-08-12**. The verification record includes DOI, title, two metadata sources, status and notes. Package-version information is maintained separately as a dated software snapshot because CRAN metadata is time-dependent.
